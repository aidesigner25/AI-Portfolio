01 / CREATOR OPERATIONS

YouTuber AI OS ✳

A seven-agent operating system that runs the back office of a 765K-subscriber YouTube channel

MULTI-AGENT SYSTEM

THE CHALLENGE

The creator’s channel was outgrowing his two-person team. Research, Monday.com task management, B-roll, podcast ops, channel analytics, sponsor outreach, and video performance reporting were all competing for the same few hours.

WHAT I BUILT

A team of seven Claude agents living in the client’s own Slack, so there’s no new dashboard to learn. They handle live research, Monday.com tasks, B-roll generation, podcast management, YouTube analytics and strategic next moves, live X scraping, sponsor email outreach, custom launch packages, weekly Dropbox reports, and highly specialized content planning. Nothing goes out, scrapes, or publishes without human sign-off.

MY ROLE

I designed the architecture and built the base prototype (API integration tests, agentic data flow). I directed my engineers on the production build and handled onboarding, client support, results tracking, and final documentation.

The hard part +

Months into production, the lead agent’s memory writes started failing. It looked like a capability problem, so I traced it to an expiring API key, and we added a self-healing step that renews the key and then saves new agent memories.

02 / CONTENT OPERATIONS

Agentic Real Estate Content Engine ✳

Short-form content and social management for a Canadian real estate team, produced by an AI clone of their lead broker.

CONTENT + AUTOMATION

THE CHALLENGE

The team needed to stay visible on Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube Shorts, but nobody had the hours to plan, film, edit, and post consistently.

WHAT I BUILT

A custom app that runs the whole pipeline. It clones the lead’s face and voice (HeyGen, ElevenLabs), researches topics (Claude, Perplexity), and drafts posts in the team’s voice. The client approves, rejects, or regenerates in one batch every week or two. Only MLS listing posts stay manual, since those need a direct link.

MY ROLE

I designed the product, prototyped the UI, infrastructure, and agentic flow, and spec’d it for my developer, who built it out and tuned it for launch.

The hard part +

One week after deployment, the voice and likeness clones mispronounced local town names and lacked expressive vocal delivery, contributing to a roughly 60% content approval rate. I traced the issue to speech training in ElevenLabs, added pronunciation examples for known failures, and refined the instructions for tone, naturalness, and cadence. After the corrections, the content approval rate rose to over 90%.

03 / LEAD CONVERSION

Maya AI: Voice and SMS Lead Conversion Machine ✳

Every inbound campaign lead gets called within 60 seconds and followed up/nurtured by our agentic team until they answer.

VOICE + SMS AGENTS

THE CHALLENGE

A real estate lead-gen company was paying for ad leads and losing them. Manual follow-up averaged 3 hours, and qualified leads slipped away between inconsistent touches.

WHAT I BUILT

A voice and SMS agent that calls each new lead within a minute of opt-in, qualifies them by motivation, and runs a personalized multi-touch follow-up loop. Hot leads hand off to a human instantly.

MY ROLE

Scoped, designed, and shipped it end to end, including conversation logic, failure handling, and final client handoff.

The hard part +

Early follow-up bugs let leads fall through the cracks. I added conditional routing, a two-week progressive retry loop for failed calls, fallback models, and SMS/email alerts if an integration fails. I also steered the client toward an inbound-only design after finding that unsolicited AI calling carries legal penalties.

04 / INDEPENDENT PRODUCT

Operatr ✳

A full-stack agentic app I designed, built, and secured myself based on my YouTube Operating System design.

FULL-STACK PRODUCT

THE CHALLENGE

My client work proved I could design and ship AI systems alongside engineers. I wanted proof I could build one solo and manage the full engineering process alone.

WHAT I BUILT

Operatr is a private workspace where AI agents research, draft documents, and execute tasks from plain-language requests. It’s a templatized evolution of the YouTuber OS. Each user gets a dashboard that tracks their live ROI, using their own hourly rate against hours saved per task. It runs on Next.js, Supabase, and Vercel, with multiple AI providers plus Apify, Tavily, and Google Docs/Sheets connected through secure user auth.

MY ROLE

I designed and built everything. I owned product design, frontend, backend, database creation, deployment, testing, change management, and security. I used AI-assisted coding (Claude Code, Codex) with every change reviewed and tested by me.

The hard part +

The agent kept picking the wrong tool for natural-language tasks. I logged the failures, then rebuilt the orchestration logic for which tools to call, when, and how to chain them, and iterated through regressions until my test calls and task requests worked reliably.
